## 1. Executive Summary & Conviction Score

**Conviction: MEDIUM for a pre-earnings swing (exit before the Q3 print). LOW for holding through earnings.**

INOD is a high-growth AI data-engineering name (Q2 revenue +58% YoY, adjusted EBITDA +92%, ~$240–250M cash, no debt) that has pulled back to its 50-day and 200-day moving averages (~$62 each) after a failed spike to $75. The setup is a **pullback to support confluence with a catalyst run-up** into Q3 earnings. The technical risk/reward only clears 2:1 if you buy at or below **$61.90**, which is a few cents under Friday's close. The earnings print is inside your window and this stock gaps 15–80% on results, so a stop-loss does not protect you through it.

Why not higher conviction: MACD is negative, institutions are net distributing, insiders are 100% sellers (net -$1.1M over 90 days), a Goldman Sachs at-the-market equity program was filed Aug 6, the CEO changed on Sep 30, and two customers are 71% of revenue.

## 4. Risk Factors & Invalidation Criteria

**Thesis is wrong if any of these happen:**
- A daily close below **$57.00** (support break, stop triggered).
- A close below **$60.03** before the macro window clears, combined with a failed bounce on rising volume. This is an early warning to cancel the second tranche.
- INOD fails to reclaim **$64.2** by Oct 23 while the Nasdaq holds up (relative weakness).
- Negative company news: a customer loss or volume cut, a large ATM sale or follow-on, or CEO-transition fallout. A second consecutive sequential decline in the largest customer's revenue would be especially damaging given the 37%/34% concentration.
- A hawkish surprise from Oct 14 CPI or the Oct 28 FOMC that sells off growth/AI names broadly.

**Key risks**
1. **Earnings gap risk (highest):** moves of 15–80% on prints; a stop gives no protection overnight.
2. **Unconfirmed date:** Q3 could land Nov 5, 6 or 9. Re-check once Innodata announces it; the exit date moves with it.
3. **Dilution overhang:** Goldman ATM; size and usage unknown.
4. **Insider/institutional selling** and a P/E near 47 leave little cushion on a miss.
5. **Unexplained Sep 22 spike:** the move that has now fully reversed may mean the previous rally was event-driven.
6. **Short squeeze cuts both ways:** 13.6% short interest can accelerate a rally, but 60% short volume suggests active hedging and market-making, not necessarily bearish positioning.
7. **Data quality:** price and indicator data come from aggregators; a few figures conflict between sources (cash $240.3M vs $250.4M; one site lists YoY revenue growth +9.8% where the company reported +58%). This scenario uses the company-reported figures.

**Bull case to monitor:** a Q3 beat plus a raised FY2026 guide (implied H2 bar is only ~$85M/quarter), falling short interest, and a clean break above $66–67.
