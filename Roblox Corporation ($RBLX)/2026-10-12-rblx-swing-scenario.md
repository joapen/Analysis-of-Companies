## 3. Price Levels & Execution Plan

### Plan A — Pre-earnings pullback long (exit before the Oct 29 close)
*Hold: ~8–12 trading days. Shorter than the requested 15–30; flagged deliberately.*

- **Entry Zone: $45.25 – $46.60** (EMA20 to SMA20). Reference entry **$46.00**. Do not buy above ~$46.60 and do not chase today's $48.
- **Stop-Loss: $43.00** (close basis, below the Oct 5 low of $43.14 and the Oct 1 close). Risk at the reference entry: **$3.00/share (6.5%)**. A 1.5 × ATR stop from $46.00 would be $42.37, which is similar; $43.00 is the structure-based invalidation.
- **Take-Profit Targets:**
  - **TP1: $51.30** (Sep 14 high / resistance) — sell 50%
  - **TP2: $54.50** — sell 50% (lower edge of the early-July supply zone)
  - **TP3 (stretch, only if run-up runs hot): $57.50** — trail the remainder; unlikely before the print
- **Time stop:** flat by the **Oct 29 close** regardless of P&L.
- **Risk-to-Reward Ratio** (RRR = (Target − Entry) / (Entry − Stop)):

| Entry | Risk | TP1 only ($51.30) | Blended 50% TP1 / 50% TP2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| $45.25 | $2.25 | 2.69 : 1 | 3.40 : 1 |
| **$46.00** | **$3.00** | **1.77 : 1** | **2.30 : 1** |
| $46.60 | $3.60 | 1.31 : 1 | 1.75 : 1 |
| $47.50 | $4.50 | 0.84 : 1 | 1.20 : 1 |
| $48.09 (last) | $5.09 | 0.63 : 1 | 0.94 : 1 |

  **Only entries at or below ~$46.00 clear the 2:1 blended hurdle; the trade is not valid at $48.** TP1 alone reaches 2:1 only if the entry is $45.77 or lower. If price never pulls back, there is no Plan A trade.
- **Position sizing (2% max risk; example $100,000 portfolio = $2,000 at risk):**
  - Stop-based sizing: $2,000 / $3.00 = 666 shares (~$30.6k, 30.6% of the portfolio). **Not recommended**, because RBLX gaps: it fell 9.86% on Sep 28 and rose 12.73% on Sep 14.
  - **Gap-adjusted sizing (recommended):** size so that a 10% gap through the stop still costs 2%. $2,000 / (10% × $46.00) = **434 shares (~$20.0k, 20% of the portfolio)**. Loss at the $43.00 stop ≈ $1,302 (1.3%); loss on a 10% gap to $41.40 ≈ $1,996 (2.0%).
  - Scale linearly to your actual equity. Consider entering in two tranches ($46.60 and $45.25).

- **Sizing for Plan B:** once the gap is known, risk is defined by the stop again; use the 2% rule on the real stop distance and apply the same 20% notional cap.

### Plan C — Optional defined-risk structure through earnings
Use only if you want exposure to the print without a gap-risk equity position.
- **Structure:** call debit spread (long near-the-money call, short call at a resistance-aligned strike) expiring **Nov 20**, after the print and the first drift week.
- **Sizing:** **max loss = total debit paid ≤ 2% of the portfolio ($2,000 per $100k).** Max loss is capped at the debit regardless of the gap.
- **RRR rule:** (width − debit) / debit ≥ 2, so the **debit must be ≤ one third of the strike width**. This requires live chain data; no option prices or implied volatility were pulled for this note, so strikes and expected move are left to you.
- **Reference for the expected move:** the Jul 31 reaction was −26.85%; 20-day realized volatility is ~76% annualized. Compare the chain's implied move to those before paying up for premium.
- **Equity alternative if holding through the print:** if you hold shares through the print anyway, size for a 25% adverse gap: $2,000 / (25% × $46.00) = **173 shares (~$8.0k, 8% of the portfolio)**.

## 4. Risk Factors & Invalidation Criteria

### Invalidation (thesis is wrong if any of these occur)
**Plan A**
- Daily close below **$43.00**, or a close below the Oct 8 low ($44.22) on rising volume, which breaks the sequence of higher lows.
- Failure at the ~$48.8 200-day and $51.3 resistance twice, with RSI rolling below 50 and the MACD histogram turning more negative.
- Negative pre-print news (new regulator or court action, a creator-economy or safety policy change that hits monetization, a downgrade) before entry or while holding.
- Not flat by the Oct 29 close: holding into the print converts Plan A into an unplanned gap-risk trade.

**Plan B**
- Oct 30 closes in the bottom third of its range, or fills the gap within three sessions.
- Q4 bookings guide implies a continued YoY decline, or full-year/2027 framing remains withheld.

### Key risks
1. **Earnings gap (largest):** one-day moves of −26.85% (Jul 31) and double-digit rallies on analyst notes are in the recent record. Stops do not execute at their price in a gap.
2. **Monetization reset persists:** Q3 bookings guided −14% to −18% YoY; younger U.S. cohorts and the Recommended-for-You algorithm changes were cited as drags. Bookings growth has decelerated from ~70% (Q3 2025) to 8% (Q2 2026).
3. **Legal and safety:** child-safety litigation and state actions (San Diego, Oct 5); the Q3 net-loss guide excludes contingency accruals.
4. **Competition for creators:** Google/Unity platform (Barron's, Oct 8) and other AI-native game tools.
5. **Extended bounce / rally quality:** ~35% off the low on mostly sub-average volume; Barron's headline flagged that the earnings rally could be "overly optimistic" (Sell downgrade, ~two weeks ago; firm not identified in sources reviewed).
6. **Macro:** FOMC Oct 27–28 lands the day before the print; high-multiple growth stocks are rate-sensitive.
7. **Event-driven insider supply:** continued scheduled insider sales are an overhang, not a signal on their own.
8. **Data risk:** price and indicator data are from public aggregators (S&P Global via StockAnalysis); peer and index relative strength, live options data, and refreshed short interest were not available. Fill those before trading.

### Review checkpoints
- **Oct 13–16:** does price pull back into $45.25–46.60 on lighter volume? If not, no Plan A.
- **Oct 28 (FOMC day):** reduce or close Plan A if not already done.
- **Oct 29, 3:45 pm ET:** final flatten decision for Plan A.
- **Oct 30–Nov 2:** apply the Plan B decision table.
