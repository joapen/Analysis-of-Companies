**Position sizing (2% max risk; example $100,000 portfolio = $2,000 at risk):**

## 4. Risk Factors & Invalidation Criteria

### Invalidation (thesis is wrong if any of these occur)
**Plan A**
- A daily close below **$77.40**, or a close below $78.26 on rising volume: the Oct 15 reversal was a stop run that failed.
- Failure at the $86–87 (200-day and Oct 15 high) and $88.3 resistance on two tests, with RSI back below 50 and the MACD histogram turning more negative.
- Negative pre-print news before entry or while holding: new regulatory action, a failed legislative step, a distribution-partner change, a downgrade, or a USDC redemption wave.
- Not flat by the Nov 3 close: holding into the print converts Plan A into an unplanned gap-risk trade.

**Plan B**
- Nov 4 closes in the bottom third of its range, or fills the gap within three sessions.
- Management lowers the circulation, RLDC or other-revenue outlook, or gives no Q4 reference.

### Key risks
1. **Earnings gap (largest):** Nov 4 is a pre-market catalyst. Feb 25 (+35.5%) and Sep 15 (−11.41%) show the size of one-day moves. Stops do not fill at their level in a gap.
2. **Reserve-yield and rate risk:** reserve income is the main revenue line. A falling reserve return rate (3.5% in Q2, −66 bps YoY) cuts revenue even when circulation grows. The Oct 28 FOMC lands one week before the print.
3. **USDC circulation and share:** circulation is below its March level; Morgan Stanley cut its 2027 and 2028 USDC assumptions by 33% and 44% on tokenized-deposit and money-market-fund competition.
4. **Distribution costs and competition:** Open Standard backers plan to share most reserve income; Coinbase joined that alliance while renewing with Circle on unchanged terms through 2029.
5. **Legislative and regulatory binary:** the CLARITY Act failed cloture 49–50 and the election is Nov 3; new CFTC and EBA actions can arrive any day.
6. **Crypto-market beta:** the stock traded with Coinbase and Bitcoin in August (for example, the Aug 14 group sell-off when Bitcoin fell under $63,000); a risk-off crypto tape can override Circle-specific news.
7. **Management transition:** CFO leaving by Dec 26, successor not named; guidance credibility and the call's tone matter more than usual.
8. **Insider supply and short positioning:** about $39.2M of insider sales in 90 days; short interest up 34% in a month. Rising shorts cut both ways: squeeze fuel on a good print, informed selling if the print disappoints.
9. **Whipsaw noise:** the structural stop is 0.70 × ATR and 8 of 20 recent sessions moved 3% or more, so a valid thesis can still be stopped out.
10. **Data risk:** price and indicators come from public aggregators (S&P Global via StockAnalysis); the Oct 15 bar uses the final close from the post-close quote, not the partial intraday table. Not available or not verified: SMA200 recomputation (sources differ: $86.06 vs $86.69), Q3 consensus, live options and implied move, 13F trend, the date of the short-interest snapshot, the Aug 5 reaction size, the Coinbase print date and the Oct 28 rate-path pricing. Earnings-date aggregators conflict; **Circle's own release confirms Wednesday Nov 4**.
